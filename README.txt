<div><span class="eyebrow">Who we are</span><h2>Balance is not a competition. <span class="pink">It is a shared responsibility.</span></h2>
<p>We believe every child deserves guidance, encouragement and the tools to make responsible choices. While protecting and advancing the gains made in female empowerment, BYM addresses the need for intentional mentorship of boys and young men.</p>
<p>We create practical opportunities for young males to develop healthy relationships, emotional intelligence, leadership, financial responsibility and life skills. Our work brings families, schools, mentors, cultural and faith leaders, and local institutions into the same conversation.</p>
<div class="quote">“A strong female child needs a responsible male child. A healthy society needs both.”</div>
<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:14px;margin:20px 0">
<div style="background:#fff;padding:20px;border-radius:16px;border:1px solid var(--line)"><strong class="pink">Our vision</strong><p style="font-size:.91rem;margin:7px 0 0">To raise responsible, mentored, disciplined and empowered males who contribute positively to families, communities and society alongside empowered females.</p></div>
<div style="background:#fff;padding:20px;border-radius:16px;border:1px solid var(--line)"><strong class="gold">Our mission</strong><p style="font-size:.91rem;margin:7px 0 0">To provide mentorship, leadership training, emotional support, financial literacy and life guidance while promoting balanced community development.</p></div>
</div>

<section id="objectives"><div class="wrap">
<div class="section-head"><span class="eyebrow">Our strategic objectives</span><h2>A coordinated response to <span class="pink">the mentorship gap.</span></h2><p>BYM's nine strategic objectives guide its work with young people, families and community institutions.</p></div>
<div class="cards">
<article class="card"><div class="icon">⚖️</div><h3>Complement female empowerment</h3><p>Prepare males to relate to empowered females in healthy, respectful and mutually supportive ways, without diminishing the rights or progress of girls and women.</p></article>
<article class="card"><div class="icon">📣</div><h3>Increase public awareness</h3><p>Engage parents, educators, faith leaders and local authorities on the importance of intentional mentorship and balanced development.</p></article>
<article class="card"><div class="icon">🫶</div><h3>Create structured safe spaces</h3><p>Provide supportive environments where boys and young men can discuss identity, peer pressure, emotional wellbeing and life choices without fear of judgement.</p></article>
<article class="card"><div class="icon">🧭</div><h3>Cultivate healthy masculinity</h3><p>Promote leadership as service, emotional intelligence, financial responsibility, respect, non-violence and shared domestic accountability.</p></article>
<article class="card"><div class="icon">🛠️</div><h3>Enhance livelihoods and life skills</h3><p>Build practical competence in financial literacy, communication, conflict resolution and livelihood skills suited to local realities.</p></article>
<article class="card"><div class="icon">🧑‍🏫</div><h3>Mobilise positive male mentors</h3><p>Invite professionals, clergy and male caregivers to walk consistently with young males and share experience responsibly.</p></article>
<article class="card"><div class="icon">🌿</div><h3>Strengthen recovery pathways</h3><p>Develop partnerships with appropriate rehabilitation and support services for males facing substance-use challenges, harmful behaviour or emotional crisis.</p></article>
<article class="card"><div class="icon">🏛️</div><h3>Engage cultural and traditional leaders</h3><p>Work with elders and cultural institutions to co-design mentorship approaches grounded in local wisdom.</p></article>
<article class="card"><div class="icon">🎯</div><h3>Mitigate risky behaviours</h3><p>Contribute to reducing school disengagement, substance-related harm and juvenile offending through prevention, guidance and positive opportunities.</p></article>
</div></div></section>

<section class="field" id="approach"><div class="wrap field-grid">
<div><span class="eyebrow">Our approach</span><h2>Mentorship that moves <span class="gold">beyond talk.</span></h2><p>BYM uses a community-owned model that combines regular mentorship with practical learning, family engagement and referral partnerships. The concept note identifies Busitema University Main Campus as the anchor for the pilot, with engagement extending into Busia District and the wider Busia–Malaba corridor.</p>
<div class="field-list">
<div class="field-item"><span class="check">✓</span><div><strong>Listen and understand</strong><p>Engage young people, families and local stakeholders to identify needs and build trust.</p></div></div>
<div class="field-item"><span class="check">✓</span><div><strong>Match and mentor</strong><p>Recruit and prepare mentors, then create consistent small groups and supportive relationships.</p></div></div>
<div class="field-item"><span class="check">✓</span><div><strong>Learn by doing</strong><p>Use bootcamps, discussions, field exposure and practical exercises to build everyday skills.</p></div></div>
<div class="field-item"><span class="check">✓</span><div><strong>Track progress and refer</strong><p>Monitor participation and learning, and connect young people to appropriate professional support when needed.</p></div></div>
</div></div>
